Statistics

Mean:
Mean is the average of a set of numerical values.
It is calculated by adding all values and dividing by the number of values.

Median:
Median is the middle value when data is arranged in ascending or descending order.

Mode:
Mode is the value that occurs most frequently in a dataset.

Variance:
Variance measures how far data values are spread from their mean.

Standard Deviation:
Standard deviation measures the typical spread of values around the mean.

Probability:
Probability represents how likely an event is to occur.
Its value ranges from 0 to 1.
